In VR: squeeze grip with a hand brought up to your head to hand off the bow (rifle handle-swap). An arrow always sits on the string: reach for its nock and pull the trigger to draw, let go to shoot. Throw your free hand down to jump, again in mid-air to catch yourself, and reach overhead on the way down to sink slowly. Left stick walks, right stick turns smoothly.
